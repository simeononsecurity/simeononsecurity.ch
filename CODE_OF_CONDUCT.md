# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the SimeonOnSecurity project a
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of
experience, nationality, race, religion, sexual orientation, or socioeconomic
status.

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, comments, documentation, and project-related events.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good faith while asking questions and resolving disagreements.
- Explain technical decisions with evidence and welcome reasonable feedback.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or confidential information.
- Follow the project's license, contribution guidance, and security reporting process.
- Accept correction and revise contributions when a problem is identified.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, or
publishing private information without permission. It also includes spam,
impersonation, malicious disruption of project services, and using project
spaces to promote unlawful activity.

Security research and technical disagreement are welcome when they remain within
the agreed scope and are communicated responsibly. Do not use project
discussions, examples, or infrastructure to target people or systems without
authorization.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately through
the [SimeonOnSecurity contact page](https://simeononsecurity.com/contactus/).
Include the relevant links, dates, and a concise description. Please avoid
including secrets or sensitive personal information in the initial report.

Security vulnerabilities should follow the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of being disclosed publicly.

Reports are reviewed as confidentially as practical. The project will make a
reasonable effort to protect the reporter's privacy and will not retaliate
against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject
contributions, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action in
response to violations. The response will consider the context, severity,
frequency, and impact of the behavior.

Maintainers are expected to apply these standards consistently and to recuse
themselves when they have a conflict of interest.
