What to Look For

  • Credentials in the URL. A username or password in the authority ends up in proxy logs, browser history and referrer headers. It is a finding, not a convenience.
  • Encoded characters in the path. Percent encoding hides ../ sequences from a reader, so the decoded form is worth a second look.
  • Unexpected ports. Port 8443 or 8080 on an endpoint that should be 443 often means a different service than the one you expected.
  • Token-like parameters. Anything named token, key, secret, sig or auth in a query string is recorded wherever the URL is.
