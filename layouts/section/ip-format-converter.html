Enter an address to see its equivalent forms.

Why Alternate Notations Matter

  • A blocklist that matches strings, not values, is bypassable. A filter looking for 127.0.0.1 misses 2130706433, which is the same address.
  • Short forms are widely accepted. Many parsers read 127.1 as 127.0.0.1, and 0 as 0.0.0.0.
  • The fix is normalisation, not more patterns. Parse the address into a 32-bit integer, then compare values. Pattern matching on the text always loses this race.
  • IPv4-mapped IPv6 is the same trap. ::ffff:127.0.0.1 reaches loopback and is routinely missed by IPv4-only checks.
