What Length Does and Does Not Tell You

  • Length narrows the field. A 32-character hex string is almost certainly one of a few 128-bit digests.
  • Length does not decide. MD5, MD4, NTLM and LM all produce 32 hex characters and cannot be told apart from the output alone.
  • Prefixes are stronger evidence. A value starting with $2y$ is bcrypt, and $argon2id$ is Argon2. Those self-identify by design.
  • Confirm with a test vector. Hash a known input with each candidate and compare. That is the only reliable identification.
