Reading the Number

  • 0 to 3 bits per byte. Repetitive data, such as a run of one character or a fixed padding byte.
  • 3 to 5 bits per byte. Ordinary English prose sits here, usually around 4.0 to 4.5.
  • 5 to 7 bits per byte. Structured or encoded data. Base64 lands near 6, and compiled binaries often sit in this band.
  • Above 7.5 bits per byte. Almost certainly compressed or encrypted, because both exist to remove predictable pattern.
  • High entropy is not proof. A compressed document, an encrypted archive and a packed malware sample all look the same. Entropy tells you what to investigate, not what you found.
