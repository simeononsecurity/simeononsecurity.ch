Syntax

  • Five fields in order: minute, hour, day of month, month, day of week.
  • A number matches exactly. * matches every value, a-b is a range, and a,b,c is a list.
  • Steps use a slash. */15 in the minute field means every fifteen minutes, and 1-30/5 means every five within that range.
  • The two day fields have a trap. When both are restricted, cron matches if either one matches rather than requiring both. This tool follows that rule and tells you when it applies.
