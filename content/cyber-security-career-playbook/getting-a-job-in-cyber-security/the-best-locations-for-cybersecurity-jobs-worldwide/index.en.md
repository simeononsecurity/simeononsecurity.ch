A city ranking across countries depends on how you define `cybersecurity job`, count open roles, convert pay, compare living costs, and handle work authorization. An unsourced composite score hides those choices. This guide gives you a repeatable way to compare locations for your own career.

## Set Your Eligibility First

Before comparing salary, check whether you have the legal right to work in each country. Read the government's current visa and professional-licensing rules, then check the employer's sponsorship policy. A remote posting might still restrict the country where you live or the systems you access. Some security and defense roles have nationality or clearance requirements.

## Compare Equivalent Roles

Choose one role with similar duties in each place, such as incident triage or cloud security administration. Local titles and labor-statistics categories might differ. Record the employer, role duties, location, required language, experience, schedule, and posting date. Count unique employer postings rather than copying a job-board result count that includes duplicates and old listings.

London, Singapore, Berlin, Sydney, and Tel Aviv are examples for a search exercise, not a ranked top five. Your feasible choices depend on work authorization, family, language, and the role you seek.

## Keep Pay Measures Separate

| Measure | Record before comparing |
|---|---|
| Employer pay | Currency, base salary or hourly rate, bonus, equity, and pay period |
| Taxes and benefits | Employee taxes, mandatory contributions, health coverage, leave, and pension |
| Living costs | Housing, transport, childcare, and other expenses for your household |
| Currency | Exchange-rate date and whether the rate is for a real transfer or an illustration |
| Official statistics | Occupation definition, geography, reference year, median or mean, and full-time status |

An exchange-rate conversion does not adjust for local prices. Purchasing power parity measures price-level differences for broad economic aggregates, not a guaranteed household budget. The [World Bank International Comparison Program](https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/icp/data) explains the measure. Use actual local housing and tax estimates for a relocation decision.

Do not mix a country's average wage across all jobs with a cybersecurity salary. Official occupational data, when available, need matching definitions and years. When they do not match, present the figures separately and state the limit.

## Practice

Select two locations where you could legally work. Find three current, non-duplicate postings for the same type of role in each. Build a table with posting links, dates, role duties, eligibility, pay definition, and estimated monthly costs. Show the exchange-rate date if you convert currencies. Write down one missing factor that might reverse your decision. Recheck all terms before accepting an offer or moving.
