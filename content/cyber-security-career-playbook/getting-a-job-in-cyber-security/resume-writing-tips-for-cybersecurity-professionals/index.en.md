A cybersecurity resume should show **scope, action, evidence, and result**. Tailor the wording to the posting without claiming tools or work you did not perform.

## Start With the Role

Highlight repeated duties in the posting. Use the employer's exact term only when it truthfully matches your experience. A keyword belongs on your resume when you are ready to explain where, why, and how you used it.

Do not paste hidden keywords, repeat a tool list, or copy duties as achievements.

## Write Evidence-Based Bullets

Weak bullet:

```text
Responsible for security monitoring and incident response.
```

Stronger workplace bullet:

```text
Triaged endpoint alerts across 120 managed devices, documented evidence and escalation decisions in the ticket queue, and reduced incomplete handoffs from 18 to 5 per month.
```

Stronger lab bullet:

```text
Built a synthetic endpoint-alert case in a disposable VM, recorded process and network evidence, wrote an escalation note, and revised the report against a peer rubric.
```

Keep workplace and lab experience distinct. Do not invent scale or impact.

## Protect Sensitive Information

Remove or generalize:

- Customer and employer secrets
- Internal hostnames, addresses, architecture, and tool rules
- Case numbers, vulnerability details, and incident dates which identify an event
- Personal data, access tokens, and screenshots from live systems
- Classified, export-controlled, contract-restricted, or clearance-sensitive details

List clearance status only when accurate and appropriate. Do not describe protected work to prove the clearance.

## Organize the Page

Use these sections when relevant:

1. Contact information and portfolio link
2. Two-line role-focused summary
3. Skills supported by evidence
4. Experience with reverse chronological dates
5. Selected projects
6. Education and current credentials

Avoid rating bars, dense graphics, headshots, and multiple columns when the application asks for a simple text document. Follow local hiring norms and application instructions.

## Resume Exercise

Select one current posting and produce:

- A requirement-to-evidence table
- Five revised bullets
- One project entry
- One list of omitted claims and why you omitted them
- A plain-text copy to check reading order

Ask a reviewer to score each bullet from zero to two for truth, scope, action, evidence, and relevance.

**Pass condition:** Every bullet scores at least eight out of ten, every keyword has a truthful example, no sensitive detail appears, dates align, and the resume fits the requested file and length rules.
