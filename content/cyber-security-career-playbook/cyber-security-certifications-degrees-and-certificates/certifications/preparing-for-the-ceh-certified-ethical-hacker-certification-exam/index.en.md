**Prepare for CEH with the current [EC-Council exam blueprint](https://cert.eccouncil.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/CEH-Exam-Blueprint-v5.pdf).** The twenty [CEH course modules](/ceh-start/) follow curriculum topics, which differ from the exam blueprint's tested domains. Compare every objective against your study notes. EC-Council applies an exam-form cut score, so do not rely on a fixed 60% passing claim.

[Return to the Cybersecurity Career Playbook](/cyber-security-career-playbook-start/).

## Define the Lab Boundary

Use written permission that names the targets, dates, methods, test accounts, stop conditions, emergency contact, and data-handling rules. Start an isolated virtual network with disposable targets and snapshots. Do not test a public service or shared cloud asset solely because you control an account. Provider rules and third-party rights still apply.

Record the initial target version and snapshot. Practice discovery, service analysis, one approved vulnerability validation, and remediation verification. Keep commands, outputs, timestamps, and scope decisions in a report. A failed test is useful evidence when you explain its cause and limits. Restore the target and confirm that no test account or altered service remains.

## Test Your Understanding

For each finding, state the asset, affected version, observed behavior, impact, confidence, and repair. Separate a scanner suggestion from a validated finding. Ask another learner to reproduce the result using your instructions without expanding scope. Revisit modules where the report lacks evidence.

Check the [CEH certification handbook](https://cert.eccouncil.org/) for current eligibility, delivery, and score policy. Use the [CEH practice test](/ceh-practice-test/) as one review tool. Do not treat a quiz score as authorization or proof of safe field work.
