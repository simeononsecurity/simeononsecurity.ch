**Each certification provider defines its own renewal units and evidence rules.** A general education CEU can mean ten contact hours in some settings. Do not apply that conversion to CompTIA, ISC2, or EC-Council credentials without checking the provider's policy.

[Return to the Cybersecurity Career Playbook](/cyber-security-career-playbook-start/).

| Provider | Unit | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| [CompTIA](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/resources/ce/) | Continuing Education Unit, CEU | Qualifying activity, relevance, documentation, required total, and renewal cycle |
| [ISC2](https://www.isc2.org/members/cpe-opportunities) | Continuing Professional Education credit, CPE | Group, activity type, annual and cycle requirements, evidence, and audits |
| [EC-Council](https://cert.eccouncil.org/ece-policy.html) | EC-Council Continuing Education credit, ECE | Approved activities, submission rules, and cycle requirements |

## Keep a Renewal Record

Create one private row per activity with the date, provider, credential, activity title, topic, duration, claimed units, rule used, and evidence location. Keep completion certificates, agendas, or publication links when required. Submit according to the provider's current portal rules. Do not claim the same number of credits across providers without checking each policy.

For example, if you finish a two-hour course, record two hours of attendance. Then check each provider's current formula and cap before entering any credit. The general ten-hour CEU convention does not determine a CompTIA award. ISC2 uses CPE, short for Continuing Professional Education. EC-Council uses ECE.

## Check Before the Deadline

Review the exact credential status, renewal date, required balance, fees, and audit requirements in the provider account. Confirm whether a higher certification renews a lower one before assuming automatic credit. If an activity is rejected, preserve the rejection and submit qualifying evidence or another accepted activity while time remains. Record the provider's final acceptance, not only your own spreadsheet total.
