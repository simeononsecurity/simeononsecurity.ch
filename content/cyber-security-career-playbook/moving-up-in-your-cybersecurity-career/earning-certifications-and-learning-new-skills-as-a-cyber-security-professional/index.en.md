A certification is one learning and assessment route. Projects, mentoring, rotations, courses, and supervised work might close the same gap with stronger job evidence.

## Measure the Gap

Collect ten current postings for a target role. Count repeated tasks, credentials, education, and experience. Separate required from preferred.

For each repeated task, mark proved, practiced, missing, or blocked. Choose one missing task which appears in at least three postings.

## Compare Learning Options

| Option | Best use | Evidence to require |
|---|---|---|
| **Certification** | Employer filter or structured body of knowledge | Current objectives, assessment, renewal, applied artifact |
| **Project** | Bounded technical or analytical task | Starting state, result, failure case, review |
| **Course** | Guided instruction and practice | Syllabus, lab, assessment, saved output |
| **Mentoring** | Judgment, feedback, and workplace context | Goals, review notes, revised work |
| **Rotation** | Supervised work in another function | Defined tasks, owner, evaluation, handoff |

## Verify a Credential

Use the provider's current pages. Record exact name, version, objectives, experience or training eligibility, exam price, preparation cost, annual fees, renewal cycle, accepted activities, expiration, and verification date.

Do not assume a credential fits a role because it is well known. Advanced credentials often require experience and might not fit an entry plan.

## Calculate Opportunity Cost

Include study hours, exam attempts, travel, labs, subscriptions, renewal, and time unavailable for projects or applications. Compare the cost with the employer filter and skill gap.

## Decision Exercise

Create a one-page record with:

1. Target role and ten-posting sample
2. Repeated missing task
3. Three learning options
4. Full cost and completion date
5. Selected route and rejected alternatives
6. Applied artifact
7. Feedback source
8. Six-month review date

**Pass condition:** The choice closes a measured gap, uses current provider data, fits your budget and eligibility, and ends with applied evidence. Pause further credentials until you apply the learned skill and receive feedback.
