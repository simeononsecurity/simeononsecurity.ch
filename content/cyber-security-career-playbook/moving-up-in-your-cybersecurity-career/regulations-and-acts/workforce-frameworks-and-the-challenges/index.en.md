Workforce sources answer different questions:

- **NICE Framework:** What cybersecurity work and capability language applies?
- **DCWF:** How does DoD describe and code cyber workforce work?
- **DoD Manual 8140.03:** How does the qualification and management program operate?
- **Qualification matrix:** Which options apply to a specific work role and proficiency level?
- **Component guidance:** How does the responsible component implement the program?
- **Employer or contracting office:** Which requirement applies to this position now?

Do not infer a universal certification rule, experience waiver, contractor rule, or deadline from a general summary.

## Verify a Position

For one current posting or position description, record:

1. Employer and source date
2. Position title and actual duties
3. Workforce element
4. Coded work role and proficiency level
5. Component or organization
6. Current manual and matrix versions
7. Accepted qualification options
8. Experience, education, training, or assessment evidence
9. Resident qualification and continuous development terms
10. Confirmation contact and response

Ask whether the role is coded, which matrix row applies, which options the component accepts, what must exist before appointment, and what happens after appointment.

## Make the Training Decision

Compare the verified accepted options by cost, time, eligibility, renewal, job relevance, and applied output. Do not purchase a credential until the coded role and accepted path are confirmed.

## Exercise

Complete the checklist for a fictional posting with one missing code and one ambiguous certification statement. Draft three questions for the employer. Keep both fields unresolved until an authoritative response arrives.

**Pass condition:** The record separates NICE taxonomy, DCWF coding, program rules, component implementation, and employer discretion. No missing fact becomes an assumption.
