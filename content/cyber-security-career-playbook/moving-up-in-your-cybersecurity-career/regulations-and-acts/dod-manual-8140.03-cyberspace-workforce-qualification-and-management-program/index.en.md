DoD Manual 8140.03 establishes the **Cyberspace Workforce Qualification and Management Program**. Applicability depends on the coded position, DoD Cyber Workforce Framework work role, proficiency level, workforce element, component implementation, and current qualification matrix.

A person holding a certification is not automatically qualified for every position. A job title does not determine the accepted qualification path.

## Use Current Sources

Check these items on the date you make a decision:

1. Current DoD Manual 8140.03 PDF and change date
2. Current qualification matrices and standard operating procedure
3. Component implementation guidance
4. Position description and coded DCWF work role
5. Proficiency level and workforce element
6. Accepted foundational and resident qualification options
7. Continuous professional development requirements
8. Timeline, exception, waiver, or contractor terms which apply to the position

Do not copy a historical DoD 8570 chart into a current decision. Ask the hiring, training, contracting, or workforce office to confirm the accepted route.

## Position and Person Records

Keep these separate:

- **Position record:** work role, proficiency level, duties, component, and requirement source
- **Person record:** education, training, certification, experience, assessments, dates, and approval

Qualification evidence does not replace demonstrated job performance. Supervisors still need task and proficiency evidence.

## Exercise

Use a fictional position. Record the source date, component, work role code, proficiency level, workforce element, duties, accepted options, missing evidence, confirmation contact, and review date.

**Pass condition:** The record cites the current manual and matrix, separates position requirements from the person's evidence, and leaves unconfirmed component decisions open.
