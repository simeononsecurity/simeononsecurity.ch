Cybersecurity includes different kinds of work. A title such as **security analyst** often means different tasks across employers. Compare duties before choosing training.

## Compare Role Families

| Role family | Typical tasks | Useful starting point | Beginner evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Security operations** | Review alerts, investigate events, escalate incidents | Networking, systems, logs | Synthetic alert report |
| **Security engineering** | Design and operate controls, identity, hardening, automation | Administration, networking, scripting | Tested hardening change |
| **Application security** | Review design and code, test builds, support fixes | Software development and HTTP | Fixed sample defect with test |
| **Cloud security** | Review identity, network, configuration, logs, and data paths | One cloud platform and infrastructure basics | Read-only posture review |
| **Governance, risk, and compliance** | Define requirements, assess risk, test controls, manage evidence | Business process, writing, audit | Fictional control test record |
| **Incident response and forensics** | Coordinate response, preserve evidence, analyze scope, support recovery | Operations, logs, evidence handling | Tabletop timeline and decision log |
| **Security architecture** | Translate requirements into system boundaries and control decisions | Broad engineering experience | Threat model and trade-off record |
| **Operational technology security** | Protect industrial environments under safety and availability limits | Networking, process knowledge, vendor constraints | Passive architecture review |

One person rarely owns every task. Legal, privacy, human resources, safety, IT, engineering, and business owners keep their own authority.

## Use NICE Carefully

The NICE Framework describes work through **Work Roles, Competency Areas, and Task, Knowledge, and Skill statements**. The Components change separately from NIST SP 800-181 Rev. 1. Check the current version before mapping a plan.

A NICE Work Role is a grouping of work. It is not a job title, staffing formula, certification mandate, or control framework.

## Try Three Paths

Choose three role families. For each one:

1. Collect three current postings.
2. Record five repeated tasks.
3. Complete one 60-minute paper or disposable-lab exercise.
4. Save one artifact.
5. Record what you liked, disliked, and could not yet judge.

Examples include a synthetic alert report, a risk register entry, a network trust-boundary diagram, or a small secure-code test.

**Pass condition:** Your comparison uses nine dated postings, three distinct artifacts, and one stated limit for each path. Select a primary path only after the work sample, not from the title alone.
