“Cybersecurity expert” is too broad to describe one job. Security work splits across role families with different authority, evidence, and outputs.

## Compare Weekly Outputs

| Role family | Common output | Escalation point |
|---|---|---|
| **Security operations** | Alert record, timeline, escalation, detection feedback | Uncertain scope, business impact, or response authority |
| **Incident response** | Decision log, evidence record, containment and recovery plan | Legal, privacy, safety, insurer, executive, or regulator issue |
| **Security engineering** | Design, configuration, test, change, rollback | Production risk or conflict with service requirements |
| **Application security** | Threat model, code finding, reproduction, fix verification | Release risk or unresolved design issue |
| **GRC** | Risk record, policy, control test, exception, assessment | Risk acceptance or legal interpretation |
| **Threat intelligence** | Source-rated report, confidence statement, relevance assessment | Attribution claim or operational response |
| **Forensics** | Acquisition record, timeline, analysis, custody record | Evidence handling or legal process |
| **Security leadership** | Priorities, service goals, staffing, budget, risk decisions | Enterprise risk or cross-functional conflict |

Dark-web access, evidence collection, scanning, and law-enforcement contact require authorization and controlled procedures. A junior learner should use synthetic cases or supervised work.

## Junior and Senior Ownership

A junior practitioner often gathers evidence, follows a playbook, documents work, and escalates. A senior practitioner handles ambiguous decisions, system trade-offs, cross-team coordination, and review. Job scope varies by employer.

## Shadowing Exercise

Interview or observe one practitioner with permission. Do not request confidential details. Record:

1. Three recurring outputs
2. One decision the role owns
3. One decision it escalates
4. Evidence used to judge quality
5. One beginner task which fits supervision
6. One work condition which surprised you

If shadowing is unavailable, use three current postings and public role descriptions.

**Pass condition:** Your one-page role brief names outputs, ownership, escalation, evidence, and an entry task. It must avoid confidential or personal information.
