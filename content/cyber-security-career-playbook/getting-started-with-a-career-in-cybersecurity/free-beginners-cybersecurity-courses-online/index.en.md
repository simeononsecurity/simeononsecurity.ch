The word **free** has several meanings. A course might offer free reading, a limited module, an audit route, a trial, or full access. Labs, certificates, grading, and retakes often follow different terms.

## Verify the Exact Offer

Record this information from the current course page:

- Exact title and provider
- Verification date
- Fully free, free audit, trial, or limited module
- Account and identity requirements
- Region and age limits
- Prerequisites
- Lab access and reset limits
- Certificate or completion record
- Cancellation steps and later charges
- Data collection and public-profile defaults

Avoid broad provider claims. Terms differ across courses on the same platform.

## Build a Low-Cost Route

Use primary public resources for concepts, then add practice:

1. **Work roles:** Review the current NICE Framework and pick one role family.
2. **Risk outcomes:** Read the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 overview.
3. **Defensive priorities:** Review CISA's Secure Our World and KEV resources.
4. **Platform skills:** Follow official documentation for one operating system or cloud service.
5. **Practice:** Use an owned disposable VM, a paper case, or a provider sandbox with clear scope.

Course completion does not equal a professional certification or workplace experience. Label each outcome accurately.

## Course Comparison Exercise

Compare three exact courses. One must be fully free or based on public primary material. Score each from zero to two for:

- Role relevance
- Current version and update date
- Clear prerequisites
- Safe practice environment
- Saved learner artifact
- Feedback or answer reasoning
- Accessibility
- Privacy and cancellation clarity

**Pass condition:** Select a course scoring at least twelve out of sixteen with no zero for safety, privacy, or role relevance. Name the artifact you will finish within 30 days.

## Maintain the List

Recheck the exact link, price, access, and lab terms before recommending a course. Remove dead links. Mark paid conversions clearly. Keep a no-cost alternative for every route.
