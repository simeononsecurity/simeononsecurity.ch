A useful community offers clear rules, active moderation, relevant discussion, and a safe reporting route. A badge or post count does not prove professional ability.

## Evaluate the Space

Record the community owner, current URL, cost, conduct rules, moderation process, recent activity, advertising policy, and account deletion terms. Read several recent discussions before joining.

Avoid spaces which normalize stolen data, malware distribution, harassment, unauthorized access, or paid recruitment scams.

## Protect Yourself

- Use only the personal details needed for the purpose.
- Keep employer, client, school, and household details private.
- Do not post live logs, internal diagrams, tokens, or incident data.
- Do not open unknown files or run copied commands without review.
- Confirm jobs and events through the organization's known website.
- Follow responsible disclosure and platform scope rules.

Choose a pseudonym where appropriate. Do not impersonate credentials or experience.

## Contribute at Your Level

Start with a documentation correction, sourced answer, event note, reproducible lab observation, or peer review. Ask a focused question after showing what you checked.

## 30-Day Plan

1. **Week 1:** Review rules and privacy settings.
2. **Week 2:** Post one sourced answer or question.
3. **Week 3:** Complete one small contribution.
4. **Week 4:** Request feedback on one safe artifact and revise it.

Keep a private log with date, link, contribution, feedback, and follow-up. Store no sensitive details about other members.

**Pass condition:** Complete four useful interactions, receive or provide one substantive review, revise one artifact, and trigger no privacy or conduct issue.
