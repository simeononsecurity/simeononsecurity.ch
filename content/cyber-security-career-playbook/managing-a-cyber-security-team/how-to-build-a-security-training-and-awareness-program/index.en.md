A learning program should reduce defined risks and improve useful behavior. Completion rates alone do not show safer work.

## Analyze the Audience

Group learners by task, access, risk, language, accessibility need, work location, and legal or labor constraints. Include contractors and leaders where the scope requires them.

Build an audience matrix:

| Audience | Risk decision | Desired behavior | Evidence | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **All workers** | Suspicious message | Report through approved channel | Report time and useful context | Accessible quick guide |
| **Developers** | Dependency or secret | Stop release and follow review route | Ticket and corrected build | Secure coding lab |
| **Managers** | Incident impact | Escalate and support response | Decision log | Tabletop exercise |

## Design the Program

For each module, state one behavior, one practice task, one feedback method, and one follow-up decision. Keep policy, privacy, and reporting routes specific to the organization.

Phishing simulations need legal, privacy, labor, and accessibility review. Avoid public embarrassment, deceptive collection of real passwords, or punitive ranking. Give learners a safe reporting path and explain how data is used and retained.

## Measure Behavior

Useful measures include reporting rate, time to report, report quality, repeat error pattern, help requests, and completion of role tasks. Record false positives and context. A higher report count might reflect better reporting rather than more attacks.

Compare a baseline with later periods or a planned comparison group where appropriate. Do not claim one training event caused an outcome without a sound design.

## Program Exercise

Create a one-page plan for a fictional organization:

1. Three audiences
2. One risk and behavior for each
3. Accessible delivery options
4. One safe practice activity
5. Baseline and target
6. Privacy and retention rule
7. Review date and improvement decision

**Pass condition:** Each audience has a role-relevant behavior, practice task, evidence measure, support route, and decision triggered by the result.
