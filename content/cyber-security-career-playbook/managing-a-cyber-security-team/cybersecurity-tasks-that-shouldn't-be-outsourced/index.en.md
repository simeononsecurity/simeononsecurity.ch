Organizations outsource many security tasks. They still need **retained accountability** for business risk, legal duties, vendor oversight, and decisions which affect their services and people.

## Keep Decision Rights Clear

Retain a named internal owner for:

- Risk appetite and acceptance
- Business priority and service impact
- Legal, privacy, safety, and contract interpretation
- Incident authority and external communication
- Vendor approval, monitoring, and termination
- Data ownership and access approval
- Evidence retention and regulator response

The owner might rely on legal counsel, specialists, or providers. Advice and execution do not transfer the organization's decision.

## Evaluate Execution Tasks

Score each task on:

- Data sensitivity and access depth
- Need for independence
- Response time and service knowledge
- Specialist skill and tool cost
- Provider concentration and continuity
- Evidence ownership and portability
- Subprocessor and data-location limits
- Audit and regulator access
- Exit and transition difficulty

Internal delivery is not automatically faster or safer. External delivery is not automatically cheaper or more skilled.

## Decision Matrix

| Task | Internal owner | Execution | Approval | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monitoring** | Security operations lead | Managed provider | Detection changes approved through change process | Alerts, cases, rule history |
| **Forensics** | Incident lead and counsel | Specialist retainer | Collection scope approved per plan | Custody and analysis record |
| **Risk acceptance** | Business risk owner | Internal | Named authority | Decision and expiry |

## Tabletop Exercise

Use this case: the monitoring provider reports suspicious access, then loses portal access during the investigation.

Produce a RACI and answer:

1. Who declares the incident?
2. Who authorizes containment?
3. Who contacts the provider and preserves evidence?
4. How does the organization operate without the portal?
5. Which notice duties need legal review?
6. How will evidence leave the provider at contract end?

**Pass condition:** Every decision has one accountable owner, the organization has direct evidence access, provider failure has a fallback, and exit support has a deadline.
