Use this lesson index after reading the [course start page](/software-development-career-playbook-start/). Begin with a target role and a supported workstation. Keep costs bounded. Use local or disposable environments for practice. Never place employer code, personal data, credentials, or paid cloud resources in a public exercise.

Complete the [Python Starter Lab](/files/software-development-career-playbook/python-starter/README.md) before the technical exercises. The hub's final capstone rubric uses the same build, test, review, failure, rollback, and teardown evidence.

## Ordered Sections

1. [Getting Started in Software Development](/software-development-career-playbook/getting-started-in-software-development/), produce a role decision, a tested starter program, and a learning plan.
2. [Education and Certifications](/software-development-career-playbook/education-and-certifications/), compare current paths, total cost, official terms, and target-role evidence.
3. [Getting a Job as a Developer](/software-development-career-playbook/getting-a-job-as-a-developer/), produce truthful application records, work samples, and scored interview practice.
4. [Growing as a Developer](/software-development-career-playbook/growing-as-a-developer/), produce feedback, design, contribution, and growth evidence.
5. [Managing a Development Team](/software-development-career-playbook/managing-a-development-team/), practice fair decisions, safe delivery, flow review, and management scenarios.

Completion requires the section artifacts, a reviewed work sample, passing tests, a debugging record, a delivery and rollback rehearsal, and a capstone review against the course pass gate.
