**An internship is one route to supervised work experience.** Compare paid employment, academic credit, eligibility, supervision, learning goals, accommodations, intellectual-property terms, and conversion policy before accepting. Apprenticeships, campus work, volunteer work with consent, and private projects provide other routes.

## Types of Developer Internships

| Type | Terms to Verify | Evidence Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Summer internship** | Dates, pay basis, location, eligibility, housing, and overtime | Reviewed work sample and supervisor feedback |
| **Co-op program** | School credit, enrollment effects, pay, schedule, and return terms | Multi-term learning record |
| **Part-time internship** | Hours, pay, workload, supervision, and academic conflicts | Bounded project evidence |
| **Externship or placement** | Worker classification, pay, academic terms, and employer oversight | Verified learning outcome |
| **Open-source fellowship** | Program status, stipend, eligibility, license, and mentor expectations | Accepted contribution and reflection |

## What Interviewers Look for in Intern Candidates

Interview formats vary by employer, role, and country. Ask for the stages, tools, timing, evaluation criteria, and accommodations route.

- Prepare one truthful project explanation.
- Practice the stated coding, debugging, testing, design, or behavioral format.
- Ask how school level, prior experience, and role requirements affect evaluation.

*Application dates vary by employer, country, school, and program. Record the official opening and closing dates for each target.*

## The Internship Application Timeline

| Month | Action |
|---|---|
| **Research** | Confirm official dates, location, eligibility, pay, work authorization, and accommodation route |
| **Prepare** | Tailor a truthful resume, test links, and record one reviewed work sample |
| **Apply** | Save the posting, submission date, contact route, and next action |
| **Verify** | Reject requests for fees, gift cards, equipment payments, or credentials outside the official employer process |
| **Review** | Track outcomes and adjust one part of the application after a useful feedback signal |

*Apply before the official deadline and keep the confirmation. Early application helps only when the employer reviews on a rolling basis.*

## Performing Well During Your Internship

The goal is supervised learning and truthful work evidence. A return offer is one possible outcome.

- **Manage scope.** Confirm the required outcome, review point, support, and deadline. Raise a scope risk early.
- **Ask for feedback at the midpoint, not the end.** A midpoint check-in allows you to correct course while it still matters.
- **Communicate blockers through the agreed route.** State the observed issue, what you tried, and what access or decision you need.
- **Build professional relationships with consent.** Join optional events which fit your schedule, access needs, and workplace policy.
- **Document your work.** A well-written design doc or final presentation makes your return offer case visible beyond just your mentor.

## Converting to a Full-Time Offer

Employers use different return-offer processes. Ask for written criteria and a midpoint review. Relevant evidence might include:

- Project completion and quality.
- Code review performance (did you respond well to feedback?).
- Job-related collaboration and communication.
- Manager and mentor advocacy.

If you receive a return offer: evaluate it as a real offer with market comparison, not as a guaranteed first job you must take.

- [ ] The artifact uses dated, role-specific evidence
- [ ] Claims, ownership, team credit, and unknown values are truthful
- [ ] Privacy, secrets, licenses, accessibility, cost, and safety received review where relevant
