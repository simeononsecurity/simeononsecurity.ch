**CompTIA Security+ SY0-801 (V8)** covers the decisions security administrators make about controls, threats, architecture, operations, and risk. Use this course to learn the concepts, practice making those decisions, and check your reasoning against the published objectives. The [SY0-701 course](/security-plus-start/) remains available separately.

*CompTIA recommends two years of hands-on security administration experience. The exercises here use fictional situations and do not require access to an employer's systems.*

**Exam details:** CompTIA lists a maximum of 90 questions, including multiple-choice and performance-based items, in 90 minutes. The passing score is 750 on a scale of 100 to 900. The practice bank on this site is multiple-choice and its raw score is not a CompTIA score.

## How to study

1. Read a lesson and complete its exercise before looking at the worked answer. Identify the requirement that makes one decision better than another.
2. Use the official objective list to check topics the examples do not exercise. Put an objective number beside each remaining gap.
3. Take the practice test. For missed questions and close calls, use the explanation to find the decisive fact and revisit that lesson.

## Domain 1: General Security Concepts (16%)

### [General Security Concepts](/security-plus-sy0-801/general-security-concepts/)

Choose security controls, plan a change you can reverse, and distinguish when encryption, hashing, and signatures meet different requirements.

## Domain 2: Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Attacks (24%)

### [Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Attacks](/security-plus-sy0-801/threats-vulnerabilities-and-attacks/)

Connect an observed indicator to plausible explanations, separate an attack path from its impact, and recognize risks in AI-assisted workflows.

## Domain 3: Security Architecture (19%)

### [Security Architecture](/security-plus-sy0-801/security-architecture/)

Assign responsibility in different environments, protect data across trust boundaries, and design recovery around measured time and data-loss limits.

## Domain 4: Security Operations (27%)

### [Security Operations](/security-plus-sy0-801/security-operations/)

Prioritize work from evidence, contain an incident without losing sight of business needs, and verify that remediation worked.

## Domain 5: Security Program Management and Oversight (14%)

### [Security Program Management and Oversight](/security-plus-sy0-801/security-program-management-and-oversight/)

Record risk decisions, evaluate third-party controls, and distinguish governance, compliance, audits, and awareness work.
