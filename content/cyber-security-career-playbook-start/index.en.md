This playbook links the English lessons on cybersecurity roles, training, job searches, and team leadership. Start with a target role. Compare its duties with current postings and official work-role descriptions. Use the exercises in each lesson to record what you learned.

## How to use the playbook

1. Choose one role and record five recent postings from one location.
2. Identify repeated duties, eligibility rules, and skills you need to practice.
3. Complete one safe, authorized exercise and save the output with its limits.
4. Compare training costs and credentials with the role requirements before paying.
5. Review your evidence with another person and revise your plan.

The [NIST NICE Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/applied-cybersecurity/nice/nice-framework-resource-center) describes cybersecurity work roles and tasks. A job title might combine tasks from more than one role. The [U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics information security analyst profile](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/information-security-analysts.htm) reports national occupation data. Its projections do not count local entry-level openings.

This playbook does not promise employment or a fixed salary. Check current employer requirements, credential rules, and local costs for your decision.

## Completion check

Make a one-page plan with your target role, five current postings, two repeated duties, one finished practice artifact, one training choice, and two unanswered questions. For each factual market claim, record the source date, geography, and population. Revisit the plan when the postings or credential objectives change.
